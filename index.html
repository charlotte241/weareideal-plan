Twelve properties from 25 landlord conversations a month is one signing for every seven conversations. Across 3.5 months that is 88 conversations producing 12 management contracts — a 14% conversion rate on outbound to landlords who do not know you yet. At 25 conversations a week the same 12 properties need only a 3% conversion, which is comfortable. Lisa offered 25 a week or 100 a week on the call. Either the conversation count goes up, or the property count comes down to six. It cannot stay as it is.
